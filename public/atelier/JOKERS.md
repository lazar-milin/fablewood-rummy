# Mystical jokers

Four additional cards, separate from the 52-card suit deck. All share a Twilight rose palette, a small crescent moon and uppercase JOKER lettering. Mirrored amethyst leaf and bellflower side motifs; no bottom flourishes. Exact colors: joker-palette.json. Reusable frame: /art/card-kit/assets/frames/joker-capsule.svg. Pixel moon / favicon: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/moon-pixel.svg (original PNG: moon-pixel.png). Side motif: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/purple-side.svg (original PNG: purple-side.png). SVG files are alpha-bounded layout windows containing the unchanged generated PNG sprites. Detail prompts: JOKER-DETAIL-PROMPTS.json.

- Emerald Dragon: cards/joker-dragon.html; transparent illustration: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/dragon.png.
- Opal Unicorn: cards/joker-unicorn.html; transparent illustration: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/unicorn-equine.png.
- Ember Phoenix: cards/joker-phoenix.html; transparent illustration: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/phoenix.png.
- Golden Griffin: cards/joker-griffin.html; transparent illustration: /art/card-kit/assets/jokers/griffin.png.

Created with the built-in image generator. Exact prompts are in JOKER-PROMPTS.json.
